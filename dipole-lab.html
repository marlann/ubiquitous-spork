Exact fields use the point-charge formula Ē = (1/4πε₀)·q/|r|²·r̂ for each charge, then add them as vectors. 1/4πε₀ = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²/C². The field arrows' tails sit at the observation point. Background arrows show direction only. Arrows longer than 9 cm are clipped and marked with a bar. Potential V = Σ (1/4πε₀)·q/r with V = 0 infinitely far away; contours are traced with marching squares on a 4-px grid.

About This Club

This club grew from a simple question: who taught you that you belong in the story of knowing? We read books that name many ways of understanding the world — through stars, roots, stories, equations, poems, and protest. Every reader brings a thread. Together, we weave something bigger.

Who It's For

Young readers (ages 8 and up) who hold multiple, layered identities and want a community that sees all of them.

How It Works

Read at your own pace. Show up when you can. Share what the book opened in you — not just what happened in the plot.

Our Promise

Your ideas matter here. You do not have to shrink yourself, explain yourself, or perform expertise. You just have to be curious.

The Theme

Every book on our list asks — in its own way — how humans and communities have learned to make sense of the world around them.

The Book of Knowers

This project began when a nine-year-old wrote a letter to Benjamin Banneker.

Every reader is also a knower. This is a place for kids in the club to share their own writing — a poem, an observation, a question, a reflection — collected into our own living book. Coming soon.
